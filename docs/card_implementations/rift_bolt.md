# Rift Bolt

## Oracle text

```
Rift Bolt   {2}{R}
Sorcery
Rift Bolt deals 3 damage to any target.
Suspend 1—{R} (Rather than cast this card from your hand, you may pay {R} and
exile it with a time counter on it. At the beginning of your upkeep, remove a
time counter. When the last is removed, you may cast it without paying its mana
cost.)
```

## Engine work — the Suspend lifecycle (CR 702.62)

Mechanics added (general): **suspend** — a full alternate-cast lifecycle reusable by any
Suspend card. Suspend is *not* an alternative casting cost (it doesn't cast the spell); it is a
special action from the hand that exiles the card with time counters, an upkeep triggered
ability that removes one, and a "when the last is removed, cast it for free" triggered ability
(CR 702.62a's three abilities).

### Behavioral decisions

- **Timing of the suspend special action.** Offered from hand whenever the card "could begin to
  be cast" (CR 702.62a) — i.e. the same sorcery/instant timing window a normal cast uses
  (`can_cast_now`), gated by cast prohibitions (702.62c) and affordability of the suspend mana
  cost. Rift Bolt is a sorcery, so it is offered at sorcery speed. No legal *target* is required
  at suspend time — targets are chosen only later, when the card is cast (702.62a third ability).

- **How time counters are stored on an exiled card.** A suspended card is in the exile zone and
  is **not a permanent**, so its counters can't live in `Permanent::counters` (the `get/add_counters`
  API only reaches permanents). They are tracked in `cur_game.suspend_time_counters`, keyed by the
  exiled card entity. "Suspended" (702.62b) = present in that map with a positive count and still
  in exile.

- **The upkeep removal is a genuine triggered ability.** On `UPKEEP_BEGAN`,
  `check_triggered_abilities` produces one `SuspendTick` trigger per suspended card the upkeep
  player owns, placed on the stack (opponents get a response window) exactly like the Impending
  end-step shed — not applied inline.

- **The free cast at zero.** When `SuspendTick` removes the last counter it grants the owner a
  FREE `from_suspend` impulse-cast permission — the same free-cast-from-exile machinery Ugin's -11
  uses (`cur_game.impulse_cast_permission`, resource `FREE`). The casting path then offers "Cast
  Rift Bolt (from exile, no cost)"; the spell goes on the stack and its target is chosen then
  (CR 702.62d / 601.2b), resolving as a normal spell for 3 damage. If the owner never casts it
  the permission lapses at cleanup and the card stays exiled, matching "if you don't, it remains
  exiled."

  Two small, intentional simplifications vs. the letter of CR 702.62a: (1) the "remove last
  counter" and "then cast" are collapsed into one `SuspendTick` trigger rather than a reflexive
  second trigger; (2) the free cast is offered at any priority window the caster holds for the
  rest of that turn (the `from_suspend` timing bypass) rather than only in the single
  trigger-resolution window. Neither is observable for a burn spell, and both keep the
  implementation on the existing, well-tested impulse-cast path.

## Tests

- **Isolation (test harness), full lifecycle** — `--battlefield-a Mountain --hand-a "Rift Bolt"`,
  `--play "A:keep,B:keep,A:desc:Suspend Rift Bolt,B:play:Forest,A:cast:Rift Bolt,A:target:Player B"`:
  - Turn 1: A pays {R} and suspends Rift Bolt → exiled with 1 time counter.
  - Turn 3 (A's next upkeep): the `SuspendTick` trigger removes the counter (0 remaining) and
    grants the free cast.
  - A casts Rift Bolt (from exile, no cost), targets Player B → **3 damage, B 20 → 17.**
- **Normal cast still works** — `--battlefield-a Mountain,Mountain,Mountain --hand-a "Rift Bolt"`:
  the hand offers both "Cast Rift Bolt" ({2}{R}) and "Suspend Rift Bolt"; casting normally deals
  3 (B 20 → 17). Suspend is a true alternative, not mandatory.
- **CI gate** — `ci_check.py --tier pygen,vocab,smoke` (codegen sync, vocab coverage, deterministic
  smoke): 0 errors, no draws.

## Result

Implemented. Suspend is a general, reusable keyword lifecycle; Rift Bolt is the first card to use it.
